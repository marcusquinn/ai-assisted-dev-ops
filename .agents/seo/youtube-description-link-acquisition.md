# YouTube Description-Link Acquisition

Test whether a relevant, disclosed link and accurate product description added to
an existing YouTube video that already ranks for a target query improves discovery,
referral traffic, indexing, rankings, or AI-search citations. This is an experiment,
not a promised ranking tactic.

## Ownership Boundaries

| Owner | Responsibility |
|-------|----------------|
| SEO | Target query, ranked-video qualification, baseline, experiment design, and outcome analysis |
| Content / YouTube topic research | Current video-result evidence, audience fit, and content context |
| Marketing-Sales | Creator contact, negotiation, compensation, disclosure confirmation, and relationship tracking |

Do not treat this as owned-video metadata optimisation; `seo/video-seo.md` and
`content/distribution-youtube-optimizer.md` own metadata for videos we publish.

## Experiment Workflow

1. **Set the hypothesis**: name one target query, landing page, audience, locale,
   and primary outcome before outreach. Record current index status, rank, referral
   traffic, conversions, and AI citation observations with capture dates.
2. **Find current candidates**: verify that each video itself appears for the
   target query in current Google and/or YouTube results. Views, channel size, or
   topical similarity alone do not establish ranking relevance.
3. **Qualify fit**: prefer a stable, relevant video whose audience and surrounding
   claims match the landing page. Reject misleading context, unrelated bulk-placement
   inventories, fabricated traffic, or requests to conceal compensation.
4. **Propose a transparent placement**: offer payment for a clearly disclosed,
   contextual description addition without requiring ranking credit. Marketing-Sales
   owns creator communication and commercial terms; do not send or spend without the
   user's normal outreach and budget authority.
5. **Supply useful copy**: place one concise, factual sentence near the destination
   link explaining what the resource does and who it helps. Use natural target-query
   language only when accurate; do not dictate unsupported claims or keyword stuffing.
6. **Qualify the link**: require an adjacent sponsorship disclosure and request
   `rel="sponsored"` (or `rel="nofollow"`) where the publishing surface permits it.
   Do not negotiate for an unqualified link that passes ranking credit.
7. **Measure separately**: compare indexing latency, rank movement, YouTube referral
   sessions, conversions, and dated AI Overview/answer-engine citations. Use a control
   query, page, or comparable untreated period where practical.
8. **Review after a defined window**: retain, revise, or stop based on measured value.
   A single before/after movement is correlation, not proof of causation.

## Decision Record

For each placement, record:

```text
Query / locale / capture date:
Video URL and observed rank:
Landing page:
Audience and contextual fit:
Placement copy and disclosure:
Compensation and approval:
Baseline metrics:
Review dates and outcomes:
Confounders / confidence:
Decision: retain | revise | stop
```

Do not report a qualified sponsored or nofollow placement as a conventional
PageRank backlink win. Report referral, discovery, citation, conversion, and ranking
observations independently.

## Evidence and Policy

The experiment lead came from an anecdotal public report claiming that contextual
links in descriptions of already-ranking YouTube videos were indexed, appeared in
AI Overview summaries, and preceded ranking and download gains. Preserve it as a
hypothesis until reproduced: https://x.com/adamlyttleapps/status/2094666545914052904

Google requires paid placements to be qualified and identifies paid links intended
to manipulate rankings as link spam:

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam

Platform, advertising, consumer-protection, and disclosure rules still apply even
when the placement is technically qualified for search.
